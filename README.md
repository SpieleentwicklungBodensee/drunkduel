# Drunk Duel (WIP Name)

Created during the Game Jam 2025 at Toolbox-Bodensee in Markdorf (Germany)
https://spieleentwicklung-bodensee.de/gamejam2025/

Topics elected were:
- Wachstum (Growth)
- Verdopplung (doubling)
- Swarm
- Out-of-control

Our team decided to focus on the out-of-control part. Plus, we wanted to have another game for our big LED Wall Display. So we came up with the idea of taking an oldschool two player game and bring add loss of control. We chose "Duel", known from old retro platforms such as Philips Videopac or Atari 2600, a game where two players each control a trigger happy cowboy trying to shoot each other. The out-of-control part is literally changing the controls one button at a time for the player who scores a hit on his opponent. The change will be announced via speech, e.g. "Player 2: Right is Triangle!" or similar. Winner will be the first player to score five kills.
